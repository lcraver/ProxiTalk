Descripti0n

"Project Arrhythmia" is a musical bullet-hell in which all things beautiful are deadly. A symphony of lights, music, pulses and blocks, Project Arrhythmia features tight bullet-hell style gameplay where up to 4 players in local co-op must navigate their way through evolving scenes and obstacles, all timed to the beat.
