"Afterbeat" is a musical bullet-hell in which all things beautiful are deadly. A symphony of lights, music, pulses and blocks, Afterbeat features tight bullet-hell style gameplay where up to 4 players in local co-op must navigate their way through evolving scenes and obstacles, all timed to the beat.

Previously known as "Project Arrhythmia."

Dodge the beat
Stages consist of mesmerizing, musically timed scenes, each one telling a story. Feel the rhythm and feel your way around the hazards! Anna will judge how you do.

Challenge your Friends
Up to 4 players supported in local co-op mode.

Robust level editor
A carefully designed level editor allows users to create their own levels and challenges using whatever music they wish.

Huge Community
A Wealth of user-created content exists, fully integrated into the game through the usage of the level editor and user-made levels. Already, 2000+ community levels have been uploaded, and more are being made daily.

Story Mode
Over 20+ hand-crafted heart-pounding levels interwoven with an engaging story.

Constant Updates
More features come with bi-weekly updates to keep the game fresh.

Set in 207X, a mysterious disease called "LNNS" has been ravaging the world. A lone AI researcher, Anna Schroeder, attempts to find the cure with her trusty supercomputer companion HAL. Stumbling upon various "Projects," the duo decides to investigate the efficacy of a cure using a pulse/rhythm-based interface. Work in progress.

Vitamin Games is an indie game development and design studio run by Pidge. Vitamin Games' current game development project is called Afterbeat, a rhythmic dodge-em up.
